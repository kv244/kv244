# Julian Petrescu

Business development in data platforms. Hands-on exploration of what AI makes possible.

Based in Singapore, I work across APAC at the intersection of enterprise technology, partnerships and healthcare. Outside work, I build software in domains well beyond my day-job expertise, from audio DSP and GPU computing to embedded systems.

I use AI coding tools, including Claude, to explore unfamiliar territory and turn ideas into working applications. I'm interested not just in what these tools can generate, but in the engineering needed to make the results useful: testing, real-time constraints, performance and deployment.

## Selected projects

- **[NeonSynth](https://github.com/kv244/Synth)**: A C++17/JUCE synthesizer, available as a VST3 plugin and standalone app, developed with Claude's assistance. Features include granular synthesis, SIMD-optimized grain processing, a sample-accurate sequencer and real-time-safe sample hand-off, supported by automated tests and release validation. Audio DSP is outside my professional domain; this project is an exploration of how far AI-assisted development can take a technically curious builder.
- **[MLTrain / Connect 4](https://github.com/kv244/MLTrain)**: An AlphaZero-style game AI trained through self-play using PyTorch and CUDA, with batched Monte Carlo Tree Search and multiple inference backends. Play it at [c4star.com](https://www.c4star.com).
- **[RingModulator](https://github.com/kv244/RingModulator) and [VolterraFilter](https://github.com/kv244/VolterraFilter)**: Further explorations in audio processing, including a JUCE ring modulator for VST3 and standalone use.
- **[TDCuda](https://github.com/kv244/TDCuda) and [sdr-cuda](https://github.com/kv244/sdr-cuda)**: GPU experiments spanning TouchDesigner particles and CUDA analysis of software-defined radio data.
- **[toyos](https://github.com/kv244/toyos), [TI-Board](https://github.com/kv244/TI-Board) and [ARM_KVP](https://github.com/kv244/ARM_KVP)**: Embedded experiments, including a toy RTOS for Arduino, work with a TI C2000 DSP board and a key-value database for Arduino UNO R4.
- **[ByteCode](https://github.com/kv244/ByteCode)**: An AI-assisted bytecode compiler experiment.
